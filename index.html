1. Structural Framework

2. Actions Right Now

3. Temporary Situations & Habits

4. Definite Future Plans

Fixed future arrangements with a clear time or location (tomorrow, at 7 p.m.).

5. Irregular & Annoying Habits

6. Historic Present & Stative Verbs

Storytelling background scenes and the rule restricting mental/possession verbs (know, own).
